Ride messaging terms

MVBRI offers optional service messages about your ride. To receive texts, select text updates in the booking form or update-preference page and check the SMS consent box. Selecting email authorizes email updates for that ride. You can also select no automated updates.

Messages may include booking and quote updates, responses to ride questions, payment confirmation, and post-ride receipt and optional tip links. Message frequency varies with the ride and your conversations with MVBRI. Message and data rates may apply. Giving SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing or requesting transportation.

Stopping texts and getting help

Reply STOP to stop text messages. You may receive a confirmation from the messaging service. Reply HELP for support, or call (346) 557-5796 or email mvbriofficial@gmail.com. After opting out, reply START from your phone if you want to resume texts.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Contact MVBRI directly about urgent ride changes. A request or quote is subject to availability and confirmation; a message delivery failure does not confirm or cancel a ride by itself.
