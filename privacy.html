Privacy notice

MVBRI uses the name, phone number, email address, route, date, time and other ride details you submit to manage requests, provide quotes and deliver your ride. Please do not enter card numbers, medical details or other sensitive information in the booking form or messages.

Ride updates

Automated updates are optional. You can choose email, text messages or no automated updates. Messages may cover your request, quote, booking status, payments, and a receipt and optional tip link after completion and verified payment. Message frequency varies with your ride. Message and data rates may apply to texts.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information needed to deliver your selected updates may be processed by our messaging service providers. We do not sell your messaging consent.

Payments and service providers

Booking records are stored using Cloudflare. Square securely handles payments; MVBRI does not collect card details on this site. Your booking name and email may be shared with Square to prepare a balance invoice. Email updates use our email provider; text updates use our connected business messaging provider when activated. Private customer information and messages are restricted to authorized business access.

Your choices

Use the preference link in an email to change or stop automated ride updates. Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. Contact us to ask about your booking information or communication preference. Declining automated messages does not prevent you from requesting a ride.

Customer reviews are published only with permission and owner moderation. Published reviews exclude booking references and contact details.
